My process

Design is a loop, not a line.

Four steps I return to on almost every project.

1

Research

I start by asking who this is for and what's actually breaking - assumptions don't survive here.

2

Sketch

Fast, low-fi, and ugly on purpose. Sketching lets me try ten directions without falling in love with one.

3

Design

In Figma I turn the winning idea into real screens with honest typography, spacing, and reusable components.

4

Test

Putting work in front of real people is where I learn the most - I iterate, ship, then start the loop again.
